## Clarification issues before bidding
- Delivery/response period 30 days (p.3) versus 90-day go-live (pp.35–36).
- Contract four years versus clauses discussing five years of support.
- Cloud cost reimbursement on p.36 versus vendor-borne cloud management on p.43.
- Affidavit stamp denomination ₹100 in checklist versus ₹500 in Annexure III.
- Performance security basis, consortium/subcontract permissibility and exact MSME relaxations.
- Confirm corrigenda on official procurement portal before submission.

## Mandatory commercial constraints
Bidder eligibility cannot be satisfied by a demo, AI agents or a newly created repository. Tender calls for company experience, staff, solvency, corporate documents and on-site support.
