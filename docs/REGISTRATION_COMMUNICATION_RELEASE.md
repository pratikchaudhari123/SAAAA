# Registration, notices and feedback pilot
Tender Part C1(II), pp.15–18, Part C2(1.1), pp.20–21, Part C2(2), pp.21–22.
New evaluation functionality: administrator provisions invited applicant login (no public sign-up); configurable program window, qualification, percentage, seat limit and estimated fee; saved draft, private required JPEG/PNG images, submit, return/reject/approve with manual verification; atomic student creation and role transition. No actual payment, original PDF retention, independently verified identity, quotas across windows, full dynamic form builder, email verification, enrollment or NEP progression. Use synthetic files only. Same password and login after approval; sign in again to refresh role. No guarantee against one person using different email accounts. No real college data.
Notices, memos, news, events and tasks: scheduled publication window, all internal members/role/individual audiences; external HTTPS resource link; acknowledgments and withdrawal audit. In-app 30s refresh. Not a gallery upload or email/SMS delivery system.
Feedback: question list, category, grade (1–5) or marks (1–100), timed availability, snapshots of eligible students by assigned course, completed lecture attendance and aggregate fee payment. One response per invitation. Reports show counts and question averages after closing when at least three responses exist. No individual answer display; timing correlation is possible by privileged database operators. No anonymity guarantee. No department rankings, detailed pending-student notification or automated email/SMS. Filters are evaluation policies pending institutional approval.
Expected schema 56 campus tables (48 + 5 communications + 3 registration), 34 Django test methods (31 + 2 + 1), 14 role types. Existing migrations remain locally untracked; reconcile exact 0001 onward after verification. Latest code must pass CI and Mac acceptance. Existing four live scripts cover preceding workflows, not full new UI/browser acceptance. Test registration and communications manually using synthetic accounts. No actual GCOEA connection. Do not claim full tender compliance.
